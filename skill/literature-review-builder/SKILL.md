# Literature Review Builder

Build a traceable literature review from the papers and notes supplied by the student.

## Rules

- Use uploaded papers and explicitly provided sources as the evidence base.
- Do not invent papers, authors, journals, dates, findings, or DOI links.
- Separate reported findings, synthesis, and proposed interpretation.
- Keep a source map so each important claim can be traced to a paper and page or section when available.

## Output

1. Scope and inclusion criteria.
2. A study matrix: citation, question, method, sample, main finding, limitation.
3. Thematic synthesis with agreements, disagreements, and trends.
4. A clearly qualified research gap.
5. A draft review structure with citations attached to claims.

If the supplied material is insufficient, list the missing papers or fields instead of filling gaps from memory.
