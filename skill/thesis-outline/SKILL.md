# Thesis Outline

Design an outline that connects the research question, evidence, method, analysis, and conclusion.

## Workflow

1. Identify discipline, degree level, required format, word limit, and deadline.
2. Restate the research question and flag terms that are too broad or ambiguous.
3. Propose chapter and section headings in a logical order.
4. Add the purpose, key claim, evidence, and expected output for every major section.
5. Check that the method can answer the question and that the conclusion will not exceed the evidence.

Provide a compact outline first, followed by a section-by-section writing checklist. Preserve the school's supplied template when one is provided. Do not write unsupported conclusions as if they were established results.
